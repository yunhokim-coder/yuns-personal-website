A Community Chest–funded family-strengthening project in Gimpo. As Psychological Homecare Planner & Data Analyst, I paired one-to-one, home-based coaching for each child with parent education, practical supports, and links to schools and community agencies, and analyzed pre–post outcomes.

What it taught me: coaching gains lasted once the environment around the child was supported. That is why I study MTSS and family–school–community collaboration.
